MedPick Support

MedPick is a pharmacy medication picking assistant that uses OCR and AI to streamline medication workflows.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I scan a document?
Tap the camera icon on the home screen to capture a pick list. MedPick will automatically extract medication details.

Why are some medications showing "Unknown" location?
Medications not found in the pharmacy database will show as Unknown. Verify the medication name and strength are correct.

Can I resume a previous session?
Yes. Saved sessions appear on the home screen and can be resumed at any time.
