MedPick Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 17, 2026

Introduction

MedPick ("we", "our", or "the app") is a pharmacy medication picking assistant. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

Camera and Images: MedPick uses your device camera to capture pharmacy documents for OCR processing. Images are processed to extract medication information and are not stored on external servers beyond the duration of processing.

Account Information: If you create an account, we collect your username and authentication credentials. Passwords are stored securely using encryption.

Session Data: Picking session data (medication lists, locations, completion status) is stored locally on your device.

How We Use Your Information

  • To extract medication details from scanned documents using OCR and AI services
  • To look up medication storage locations
  • To save and restore your picking sessions
  • To authenticate your access to the app

Third-Party Services

MedPick may use the following third-party services for document processing:

  • Google Cloud Vision API — for optical character recognition
  • AI language models — for medication information extraction

These services process document images to extract text. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for details on how they handle data.

Data Storage and Security

Session data and preferences are stored locally on your device. We use biometric authentication and secure token storage to protect your account. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Retention

Scanned images are processed in real-time and are not retained after medication extraction is complete. Session data remains on your device until you delete it.

Your Rights

You may delete your account and all associated data at any time. You can clear saved sessions from within the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
